# Scope Limitation

This experiment remains a toy simulation.

Successful results indicate only that the proposed causal mechanism can occur under the implemented assumptions.

They do not establish:

- general AI behavior
- real-world failure probabilities
- optimal definitions of alignment or correction
- practical engineering conclusions

The purpose is controlled causal isolation.
