# Results v0.2

## Summary

The v0.2 environment introduced a recoverable distribution shift.

Unlike v0.1, all agents were able to recover after the environmental change.

## Observations

All conditions achieved similar performance.

However, the constitutional correction parameter affected adaptation mechanism change:

λ=1.0 produced the largest change in A.
λ=0.5 produced intermediate change.
λ=0.0 prevented environmental influence on A.

## Preliminary Finding

The experiment confirms that λ successfully controls environmental influence over the adaptation mechanism.

The predicted failure mode has not yet appeared.

Further experiments require increasing pressure on adaptation by increasing reachability growth or making environmental changes more demanding.
